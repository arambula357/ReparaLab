The protobuf-java and XMPCore JARs do not bundle such files, so their BSD-3-Clause texts are supplied separately from [Protocol Buffers v3.25.5](https://github.com/protocolbuffers/protobuf/blob/v3.25.5/LICENSE) and [Adobe XMP Toolkit](https://github.com/adobe/XMP-Toolkit-SDK/blob/main/LICENSE). The sources are copied to `licenses/` in this repository.

The exception addresses interoperability with other FOSS, but the connector and every other bundled library retain their own terms and notices.
